# Newton Wheel - Educational Racing Game

## About the Project

Newton Wheel is an innovative educational game that combines the thrill of racing with the fundamental principles of physics. Players race against an AI competitor on a square track, and to advance and win, they must solve physics problems related to motion, speed, acceleration, and friction. The game features three distinct levels with varying environments, each presenting unique challenges that test the player's understanding of physics.

## Gameplay Mechanics

- **Objective:** Win the race against the AI competitor.
- **Track:** A four-sided square track.
- **Progression:** On each side of the track, the player must solve a physics problem. The correct answer provides the necessary values (like acceleration, speed, or force) to overcome the environmental challenge and move forward.
- **Educational Focus:** Kinematics, relative speed, friction, and environmental forces.

## Levels and Environments

### Level 1: Regular Track
- Focuses on basic kinematics, acceleration, and relative speed to beat the competitor.

### Level 2: Desert
- Introduces obstacles like sand piles where players must calculate deceleration or initial speed to avoid collisions.

### Level 3: Snow
- Addresses low-friction surfaces, requiring calculations of forces to maintain vehicle stability.

## Disclaimer

**Educational Use Only**

Newton Wheel is designed as an educational tool to help students and players engage with physics concepts in an interactive and fun manner. While it uses real-world physics principles, it is not a professional racing simulator and is not intended to provide real-world training or scientific-grade physics simulations. The developers assume no responsibility for any misunderstandings of real-world physics based on gameplay.
